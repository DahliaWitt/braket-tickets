5. Resale, Waitlist & Transfers

Braket Tickets supports in-platform ticket resale only when resale is enabled for the event. A ticket holder may list a valid ticket for resale only if the ticket is eligible, the ticket holder owns the ticket, ticket sales for the event are still active, the event has not passed, and there is not already another active resale listing for the same ticket.

Resale tickets are offered only after primary inventory for the event is sold out. Listed resale tickets are generally offered in the order they were listed, excluding a seller's own listing. The resale purchase flow shows the buyer's resale price before payment. A resale listing may enter a temporary checkout hold while a buyer attempts to purchase it. If checkout expires, is abandoned, or fails, the listing may return to the resale queue.

Sellers may cancel a resale listing only while it is still listed and not already in a buyer's pending checkout or completed. Braket LLC or the Event Organizer may cancel or invalidate resale listings when reasonably necessary for fraud prevention, safety, ticket validity, event operations, legal compliance, or correction of an error.

When a resale purchase completes, the seller's original ticket is invalidated and the buyer receives a new ticket. The seller refund is issued through Stripe to the seller's original payment method and is generally the seller's original per-ticket purchase amount minus the event's resale fee. Original payment processing fees and other processor costs may be unrecoverable and may reduce the seller's refund.

Resale notification subscriptions and waitlist-style notifications do not reserve a ticket, guarantee availability, guarantee notification delivery, or create priority outside the in-platform resale flow. You must still be eligible to purchase the event when a resale ticket is available.

Unless Braket Tickets provides an explicit in-platform transfer or resale feature for an event, off-platform ticket transfers, sales, and attempted QR-code reassignment are not supported.
