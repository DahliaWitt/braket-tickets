1. Introduction

Braket LLC ("we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data. This privacy policy describes how we collect, use, and share information when you use our website and services. This Privacy Policy is designed to comply with the California Online Privacy Protection Act (CalOPPA) and explains our practices regarding personal information.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information that falls into the following key categories:

  • Identifiers: Real name, email address.
  • Commercial Information: Records of tickets purchased and event attendance history.
  • Internet Activity: Browsing history on the Braket Tickets platform, interaction data, and input patterns (analyzed to improve usability).
  • User-Generated Content: Information provided in event applications, vetting process responses, and communications you send to us.

We route frontend telemetry through Cloudflare Pages. For analytics, we attach coarse location signals such as metro area, country, and region before forwarding PostHog events. Cloudflare and our telemetry providers may also process technical network metadata as part of request delivery.

Important: We use Stripe, Inc. ("Stripe") for payment processing, including Stripe Connect for Third-Party Events. We do not store your credit card number, CVV, or bank account details on our servers — this information is collected and tokenized directly by Stripe through an embedded Payment Element. Braket Tickets retains only a Stripe payment identifier, the purchase amount, and transaction status so we can reconcile orders and process refunds. Stripe processes your payment information in accordance with its Privacy Policy (opens in new tab).

3. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We use analytics tools (PostHog) to analyze user interactions, including clickstream data and input patterns, to improve the platform. PostHog automatically captures some interactions ("autocapture") to help us understand usability. We also use Sentry to collect error reports and related technical diagnostics so we can investigate failures.

Analytics identity is limited to internal account identifiers and role metadata; we do not send your name or email address as PostHog identity properties. If you submit feedback, the message text is sent to PostHog so we can review and act on it. We may use masked session replay for quality and support, with text and form inputs hidden by default.

Sensitive checkout, authentication, vetting, QR code, ticket PDF, scanner, guest list, attendee, buyer, and roster regions are marked to be blocked from analytics capture. Replay network capture does not record request headers, response headers, request bodies, or response bodies.

We use cookies, local storage, and similar tracking technologies to track the activity on our Service and hold certain information.

Technologies We Use

  • Cookies: Small files placed on your device to enable basic features and analytics.
  • Local Storage: We use your browser's local storage to save your session and preferences.

Purposes

  • Essential (Strictly Necessary): These are required for the website to function (e.g., logging you in, processing ticket payments). You cannot opt-out of these.
  • Analytics (Performance): We use PostHog to collect data on how you interact with our website (e.g., pages visited, buttons clicked, performance errors). This data is used solely for improving user experience and is not used for cross-context behavioral advertising.

We do not use cookies for cross-site tracking or advertising.

PostHog may collect data including device type, browser information, and on-site behavior. We do not intentionally store raw IP addresses in analytics. Instead, we use derived coarse location information to understand broad usage patterns and improve the service. Our proxy code strips visitor IP headers that it controls, while Cloudflare and provider infrastructure may process transient network metadata to deliver requests. You can learn more about how PostHog handles data in their Privacy Policy (opens in new tab).

4. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to run Braket Tickets, keep ticketing and vetting moving, and let you stay in control of what kind of email you hear from us. In practice, that means:

  • To provide and maintain our Service.
  • To notify you about changes to our Service.
  • To allow you to participate in interactive features when you choose to do so.
  • To provide customer support.
  • To monitor the usage of the Service (via PostHog).
  • To investigate site errors and other technical failures (via Sentry).
  • To send community announcement emails about newly published events to vetted members who are currently opted in for that community.
  • To honor unsubscribe requests, store your email preferences, and remember which communities you do or do not want marketing emails from.
  • To measure delivery, open, and click activity for marketing emails so we can understand performance, keep messages useful, and avoid over-sending.
  • To detect, prevent and address technical issues.

5. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share your information only with the following categories of third parties to provide our services:

  • Analytics and Error Monitoring Providers: We share usage data with PostHog to analyze site traffic and usage patterns, and we share error reports plus related technical diagnostics with Sentry so we can detect and troubleshoot failures. Our proxy code strips visitor IP headers that we control, but Cloudflare and these providers may still process technical network metadata as part of request delivery.
  • Payment Processors: We share transaction data with Stripe, Inc. to process ticket purchases. This includes the purchase amount, your email address (passed to Stripe so it can send you a payment receipt), and order metadata such as the event name and ticket quantity. Stripe separately collects your payment card information directly from you through an embedded Payment Element — that information is governed by Stripe's Privacy Policy (opens in new tab) and does not pass through Braket Tickets' servers. For Third-Party Events, Stripe Connect transfers net proceeds to the Event Organizer's connected Stripe account; in that flow Stripe also makes limited transaction metadata visible to the Event Organizer's connected Stripe account.
  • Email Delivery Providers: We share the information needed to deliver Braket Tickets emails, including marketing announcements and transactional messages. For marketing emails, this can include limited engagement data such as whether an email was opened or a link was clicked, along with unsubscribe status.
  • Event Organizers: We share your name and email with the organizer of the specific event you purchase tickets for, so they can manage the guest list.
    Please note that Event Organizers are independent third parties. Once your data is shared with them for the purpose of event entry, it is governed by their own privacy policies. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of Event Organizers.

9. Do Not Track & Global Privacy Control

California law requires us to let you know how we respond to web browser Do Not Track (DNT) signals and Global Privacy Control (GPC) mechanisms.

We honor both signals. When your browser sends a Do Not Track signal or a Global Privacy Control signal, we disable passive analytics and replay collection (PostHog). No passive usage events are captured, and no analytics cookies or local storage entries are created.

If you explicitly submit the footer feedback form, we send that feedback message and limited diagnostic context to PostHog so we can receive and act on your request.

Essential cookies and local storage required for authentication, session management, and core site functionality are not affected by these signals, as they are necessary for the Service to operate.

You can enable DNT or GPC in your browser settings. Most modern browsers support at least one of these signals. For GPC, visit globalprivacycontrol.org (opens in new tab) for compatible browsers and extensions.
