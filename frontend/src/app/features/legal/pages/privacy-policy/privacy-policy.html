1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains what information Braket LLC ("we," "us," or "our") collects, how we use it, and when we share it when you use our website and services. This Privacy Policy is designed to explain our practices under the California Online Privacy Protection Act (CalOPPA) and, where applicable, the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act (CCPA/CPRA).

2. Information We Collect

We collect information that falls into the following key categories, depending on how you use the Service:

  • Identifiers and Account Information: Real name, email address, account identifiers, profile image if provided, authentication status, email verification status, and account preference records.
  • Commercial Information: Records of tickets purchased, order amounts, ticket quantities, ticket tiers, refund and dispute records, Stripe payment identifiers, and event attendance or check-in history.
  • Community, Vetting, and Event Information: Community memberships, organizer roles, event applications, vetting answers, trust or approval status, guest-list details, attendee names, QR/ticket status, and communications you send to us or to an organizer through the Service.
  • Organizer and Administrative Information: Organizer names, contact details, public organizer profiles, configured event details, Stripe connected-account identifiers, onboarding and payout-readiness status, audit records, and administrative actions.
  • Internet, Device, Reliability, and Email Engagement Data: Route or URL context associated with errors, performance traces, or support workflows; browser and device information; error diagnostics; limited Sentry replay context; first-party marketing email opens and clicks; and activity needed for essential site functions, reliability tooling, and support workflows. We minimize diagnostic detail and avoid sending sensitive checkout, authentication, vetting, ticket, or roster content to reliability providers.
  • Communications and Email Preferences: Support requests, feedback you submit, transactional email records, community announcement opt-in or opt-out state, unsubscribe records, and limited marketing email engagement such as opens and clicks.
  • Sensitive Personal Information: Braket Tickets is a community ticketing service, and some community, vetting, event, or attendance information may reveal sensitive personal information depending on what you choose to provide and which events or communities you interact with. We use this information to provide the Service, operate ticketing and vetting, support safety and access controls, prevent abuse, and comply with law. We do not use it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

3. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We do not use third-party tools for advertising, cross-site tracking, or cross-context behavioral advertising. We use Sentry for reliability diagnostics and feedback. Footer feedback is sent through Sentry Feedback so we can review and act on it; this is an explicit submission, not passive behavior tracking.

We also use Sentry for error monitoring and performance diagnostics. Depending on the failure and browser context, Sentry may collect error messages, stack traces, URLs or route templates, release and environment information, browser, device, and operating-system metadata, performance traces, console breadcrumbs, and limited network breadcrumbs. We may also forward sanitized backend and operational error logs to Sentry when needed to investigate production failures. We configure Sentry not to send default personal information, and any Sentry replay collection is sampled and masked by default: text and form inputs are hidden and media is blocked. Sentry Feedback may also include the message you submit, the email address you choose to provide, and a screenshot if screenshot capture is enabled for that feedback form. DNT and GPC signals do not disable essential error, security, and reliability monitoring.

We use cookies, local storage, session storage, and first-party email tracking links or pixels to operate the Service, remember temporary state, protect accounts, and measure whether community announcement emails were delivered, opened, or clicked.

Technologies We Use

  • Cookies: Small files placed on your device to enable basic features.
  • Local Storage: We use your browser's local storage to save your session, guest checkout state, and preferences.
  • Session Storage: We use session storage for temporary browser-tab state, such as draft vetting answers or short-lived recovery state.
  • First-Party Email Tracking: Community announcement emails may include Braket-owned open pixels and tracked redirect links so we can count opens, clicks, and unsubscribe activity for those messages.
  • Error Monitoring and Reliability: We use Sentry to investigate failures, trace performance problems, and keep the Service secure and reliable. Error monitoring is not used for advertising.
  • Embedded Media: Some help pages may include YouTube videos using YouTube's privacy-enhanced embed domain. Google or YouTube may still process technical information needed to load or play the embedded video.

Purposes

  • Essential (Strictly Necessary): These are required for the website to function, such as logging you in, processing ticket payments, preserving guest checkout state, and remembering short-lived recovery state. You cannot opt out of these.
  • Reliability and Security: Sentry diagnostics, masked replay, operational logs, and related technical metadata help us investigate failures, prevent abuse, and protect the Service. DNT and GPC signals do not disable this monitoring.
  • Community Email Measurement: Braket-owned open pixels and tracked links help us understand whether community announcement emails were delivered, opened, clicked, or unsubscribed from. You can opt out of community announcement emails through account settings or unsubscribe links.

We do not use cookies for cross-site tracking or advertising.

We do not use raw IP addresses for advertising or behavioral profiling. Our Sentry monitor tunnel strips visitor IP headers that it controls before forwarding error envelopes. Cloudflare and provider infrastructure may still process transient network metadata to deliver requests and protect the Service.

4. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to run Braket Tickets, keep ticketing and vetting moving, and let you stay in control of what kind of email you hear from us. In practice, that means:

  • To provide and maintain our Service.
  • To create accounts, verify email addresses, authenticate sessions, and enforce account preferences.
  • To process ticket purchases, issue tickets, manage guest lists, support QR-code check-in, reconcile payments, process refunds, and handle payment disputes.
  • To process community applications, organizer vetting, membership status, access controls, and safety-related decisions.
  • To support organizers and administrators, including event management, Stripe Connect onboarding status, payout readiness, audit logs, and fraud or abuse prevention.
  • To notify you about changes to our Service.
  • To allow you to participate in interactive features when you choose to do so.
  • To provide customer support.
  • To investigate site errors and other technical failures (via Sentry).
  • To send community announcement emails about newly published events to vetted members who are currently opted in for that community.
  • To honor unsubscribe requests, store your email preferences, and remember which communities you do or do not want marketing emails from.
  • To measure delivery, open, and click activity for marketing emails so we can understand performance, keep messages useful, and avoid over-sending.
  • To detect, prevent and address technical issues.

5. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information and do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. We disclose personal information to service providers, processors, and independent event organizers only as needed to provide, protect, and improve the Service:

  • Error Monitoring Providers: Explicit feedback submissions are sent through Sentry Feedback. We also share error reports, stack traces, route or URL context, browser and device metadata, performance traces, breadcrumbs, and masked replay context with Sentry so we can detect and troubleshoot failures. Cloudflare and these providers may still process technical network metadata as part of request delivery.
  • Infrastructure and Authentication Providers: We use providers such as Convex for application backend, database, file storage, and authentication infrastructure; Cloudflare for hosting, DNS, routing, error-monitoring proxying, and request delivery; and Google or Discord if you choose to sign in with or link those social login providers. These providers process information needed to provide their services, such as account identifiers, authentication status, request metadata, storage records, and technical logs.
  • Embedded Media Providers: Help pages may embed YouTube videos using YouTube's privacy-enhanced embed domain. Google or YouTube may process technical request and playback information when the embedded player loads or plays.
  • Payment Processors: We share transaction data with Stripe, Inc. to process ticket purchases. This includes the purchase amount, your email address (passed to Stripe so it can send you a payment receipt), and order metadata such as the event name and ticket quantity. Stripe separately collects your payment card information directly from you through an embedded Stripe Checkout flow — that information is governed by Stripe's Privacy Policy (opens in new tab) and does not pass through Braket Tickets' servers. For Third-Party Events, Stripe Connect transfers net proceeds to the Event Organizer's connected Stripe account; in that flow Stripe also makes limited transaction metadata visible to the Event Organizer's connected Stripe account. For organizers, Stripe may collect business, identity, tax, bank, verification, compliance, and payout information directly and may share connected-account status, payment, payout, refund, dispute, and transaction metadata with Braket LLC as the Connect platform.
  • Email Delivery Providers: We share the information needed to deliver Braket Tickets emails through providers such as Resend and, where configured for preview or critical fallback delivery, SMTP providers. This includes transactional messages, community announcements, authentication emails, ticket emails, and support-related messages. For marketing emails, Braket Tickets also uses first-party open pixels and tracked redirect links to record limited engagement data such as whether an email was opened or a link was clicked, along with unsubscribe status.
  • Event Organizers: We share your name, email, ticket status, order details needed for admission, application or vetting information relevant to that organizer, and check-in or guest-list details with the organizer of the specific event or community you interact with, so they can manage access, guest lists, event operations, and safety.
    Please note that Event Organizers are independent third parties. Once your data is shared with them for event, community, vetting, or organizer operations, it is governed by their own privacy policies. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of Event Organizers.

6. Data Security

We implement reasonable security measures to protect your information, including limiting operational access to people and providers who need it, using Stripe-hosted payment collection for card and bank details, and relying on service providers for secure hosting and transmission. No method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure.

7. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, including providing the Service, maintaining account and ticket records, honoring opt-outs, resolving disputes, preventing fraud or abuse, complying with legal, tax, accounting, and payment obligations, and enforcing our agreements.

Some records may need to be retained after an account deletion request if they are required for payment reconciliation, refunds, chargebacks, financial records, organizer audit logs, safety investigations, legal compliance, or preserving unsubscribe and marketing opt-out status. We delete, deidentify, or minimize personal information when retention is no longer reasonably necessary for those purposes.

Some current examples: unconverted guest checkout sessions are designed to expire after 24 hours and are cleaned up regularly; operational email delivery metadata and delivery-failure records are generally cleaned after about 30 days; internal email-provider component records are generally cleaned after 7 to 30 days depending on status; and administrative audit logs are generally retained for about 365 days. Marketing engagement, preference, and unsubscribe records may be retained longer so we can measure community announcement delivery and honor opt-outs.

Deletion or minimization may not immediately remove data from backups, provider systems, financial processors, error monitoring systems, or records that we must keep for legal, tax, accounting, fraud-prevention, dispute, safety, or security reasons. Provider-side records follow those providers' own retention and deletion processes.

9. Do Not Track & Global Privacy Control

California law requires us to let you know how we respond to web browser Do Not Track (DNT) signals and Global Privacy Control (GPC) mechanisms.

We do not use third-party tools for advertising, cross-site tracking, or cross-context behavioral advertising. Because we do not sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising, GPC does not need to disable any advertising sale or sharing flow.

The footer feedback form is an explicit way to send us feedback through Sentry Feedback. If you submit that form, we send the message text and any email address you provide to Sentry even if your browser sends DNT or GPC. If you prefer not to send feedback through Sentry, you can contact us directly by email at contact@braket.gay.

Essential cookies, local storage, and session storage required for authentication, session management, payment checkout, and core site functionality are not affected by these signals, as they are necessary for the Service to operate. Backend operational events, Sentry diagnostics, and first-party email engagement records may still be created when needed to keep the Service secure, reliable, and accountable for messages we send.

You can enable DNT or GPC in your browser settings. Most modern browsers support at least one of these signals. For GPC, visit globalprivacycontrol.org (opens in new tab) for compatible browsers and extensions.
