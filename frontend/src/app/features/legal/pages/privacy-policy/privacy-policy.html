1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains what information Braket LLC ("we," "us," or "our") collects, how we use it, and when we share it when you use our website and services. This Privacy Policy is designed to explain our practices under the California Online Privacy Protection Act (CalOPPA) and, where applicable, the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act (CCPA/CPRA).

2. Information We Collect

We collect information that falls into the following key categories, depending on how you use the Service:

We use this information to provide the Service, operate ticketing and vetting, support safety and access controls, prevent abuse, and comply with law. We do not use it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

3. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We use analytics tools (PostHog) to analyze usage and improve the platform. Our PostHog configuration captures page views, page leaves, and a limited allowlist of clicks or form submissions on elements we mark for analytics.

Analytics identity is limited to internal account identifiers and role metadata; we do not send your name or email address as PostHog identity properties. If you submit feedback through the footer feedback form, the message text is sent to PostHog so we can review and act on it; this is an explicit submission, not passive analytics capture. We may use masked session replay for quality and support, with text and form inputs hidden by default.

Sensitive checkout, authentication, vetting, QR code, ticket PDF, scanner, guest list, attendee, buyer, and roster regions are marked to be blocked from analytics capture. Replay network capture does not record request headers, response headers, request bodies, or response bodies.

We also use Sentry for error monitoring and performance diagnostics. Depending on the failure and browser context, Sentry may collect error messages, stack traces, URLs or route templates, release and environment information, browser, device, and operating-system metadata, performance traces, console breadcrumbs, and limited network breadcrumbs. We may also forward sanitized backend and operational error logs to Sentry when needed to investigate production failures. We configure Sentry not to send default personal information, and any replay collection is masked by default: text and form inputs are hidden and media is blocked. DNT and GPC signals do not disable essential error, security, and reliability monitoring.

We use cookies, local storage, and similar tracking technologies to track the activity on our Service and hold certain information.

Technologies We Use

  • Cookies: Small files placed on your device to enable basic features and analytics.
  • Local Storage: We use your browser's local storage to save your session, guest checkout state, feature flags, and preferences.
  • Error Monitoring and Reliability: We use Sentry to investigate failures, trace performance problems, and keep the Service secure and reliable. Error monitoring is not used for advertising.

Purposes

  • Essential (Strictly Necessary): These are required for the website to function (e.g., logging you in, processing ticket payments). You cannot opt-out of these.
  • Analytics (Performance): We use PostHog to collect data on how you interact with our website (e.g., pages visited, selected buttons clicked, feature interactions, and client-side performance signals). This data is used solely for improving user experience and service reliability and is not used for cross-context behavioral advertising.

We do not use cookies for cross-site tracking or advertising.

PostHog may collect data including device type, browser information, and on-site behavior. We do not intentionally store raw IP addresses in analytics. Instead, we use derived coarse location information to understand broad usage patterns and improve the service. Our proxy code strips visitor IP headers that it controls, while Cloudflare and provider infrastructure may process transient network metadata to deliver requests. You can learn more about how PostHog handles data in their Privacy Policy (opens in new tab).

5. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information and do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. We disclose personal information to service providers, processors, and independent event organizers only as needed to provide, protect, and improve the Service:

  • Analytics and Error Monitoring Providers: We share usage data with PostHog to analyze site traffic, product usage, and service reliability. Browser DNT/GPC signals disable passive frontend PostHog SDK capture, replay, and persistence, but explicit feedback submissions and server-side operational analytics may still send sanitized transaction, security, reliability, and business events to PostHog. We share error reports, stack traces, route or URL context, browser and device metadata, performance traces, breadcrumbs, and masked replay context with Sentry so we can detect and troubleshoot failures. Our proxy code strips visitor IP headers that we control, but Cloudflare and these providers may still process technical network metadata as part of request delivery.
  • Infrastructure and Authentication Providers: We use providers such as Convex for application backend, database, file storage, and authentication infrastructure; Cloudflare for hosting, DNS, routing, telemetry proxying, and request delivery; and Google or Discord if you choose to sign in with or link those social login providers. These providers process information needed to provide their services, such as account identifiers, authentication status, request metadata, storage records, and technical logs.
  • Payment Processors: We share transaction data with Stripe, Inc. to process ticket purchases. This includes the purchase amount, your email address (passed to Stripe so it can send you a payment receipt), and order metadata such as the event name and ticket quantity. Stripe separately collects your payment card information directly from you through an embedded Stripe Checkout flow — that information is governed by Stripe's Privacy Policy (opens in new tab) and does not pass through Braket Tickets' servers. For Third-Party Events, Stripe Connect transfers net proceeds to the Event Organizer's connected Stripe account; in that flow Stripe also makes limited transaction metadata visible to the Event Organizer's connected Stripe account. For organizers, Stripe may collect business, identity, tax, bank, verification, compliance, and payout information directly and may share connected-account status, payment, payout, refund, dispute, and transaction metadata with Braket LLC as the Connect platform.
  • Email Delivery Providers: We share the information needed to deliver Braket Tickets emails through providers such as Resend and, where configured for preview or critical fallback delivery, SMTP providers. This includes transactional messages, community announcements, authentication emails, ticket emails, and support-related messages. For marketing emails, Braket Tickets also uses first-party open pixels and tracked redirect links to record limited engagement data such as whether an email was opened or a link was clicked, along with unsubscribe status.
  • Event Organizers: We share your name, email, ticket status, order details needed for admission, application or vetting information relevant to that organizer, and check-in or guest-list details with the organizer of the specific event or community you interact with, so they can manage access, guest lists, event operations, and safety.
    Please note that Event Organizers are independent third parties. Once your data is shared with them for event, community, vetting, or organizer operations, it is governed by their own privacy policies. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of Event Organizers.

7. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, including providing the Service, maintaining account and ticket records, honoring opt-outs, resolving disputes, preventing fraud or abuse, complying with legal, tax, accounting, and payment obligations, and enforcing our agreements.

Some records may need to be retained after an account deletion request if they are required for payment reconciliation, refunds, chargebacks, financial records, organizer audit logs, safety investigations, legal compliance, or preserving unsubscribe and marketing opt-out status. We delete, deidentify, or minimize personal information when retention is no longer reasonably necessary for those purposes.

Some current examples: unconverted guest checkout sessions are designed to expire after 24 hours and are cleaned up regularly; email delivery metadata and delivery-failure records are generally cleaned after about 30 days; internal email-provider component records are generally cleaned after 7 to 30 days depending on status; and administrative audit logs are generally retained for about 365 days. Marketing preference and unsubscribe records may be retained longer so we can honor opt-outs.

Deletion or minimization may not immediately remove data from backups, provider systems, financial processors, error monitoring systems, analytics systems, or records that we must keep for legal, tax, accounting, fraud-prevention, dispute, safety, or security reasons. Provider-side records follow those providers' own retention and deletion processes.

9. Do Not Track & Global Privacy Control

California law requires us to let you know how we respond to web browser Do Not Track (DNT) signals and Global Privacy Control (GPC) mechanisms.

When your browser sends a Do Not Track signal or a Global Privacy Control signal, we use that signal to disable passive frontend PostHog analytics, session replay, and persistence. No passive frontend usage events are captured through the PostHog browser SDK, and we configure that SDK not to persist PostHog analytics cookies or local storage entries. Because we do not sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising, GPC does not need to disable any advertising sale or sharing flow.

The footer feedback form is an explicit way to send us feedback. If you submit that form, we send the message text and feedback category to PostHog even if your browser sends DNT or GPC. If you prefer not to send feedback through PostHog, you can contact us directly by email at contact@braket.gay.

Essential cookies and local storage required for authentication, session management, payment checkout, and core site functionality are not affected by these signals, as they are necessary for the Service to operate. Backend operational events and error monitoring may still receive diagnostics needed to keep the Service secure and reliable.

You can enable DNT or GPC in your browser settings. Most modern browsers support at least one of these signals. For GPC, visit globalprivacycontrol.org (opens in new tab) for compatible browsers and extensions.
