# Your account

Your profile, email, password, and social logins all live on the Account Settings page. Get there from the menu in the top right corner of any page. If you don't have an account yet, the [Getting started](./getting-started.md) guide covers that.

## Display name

This is what other people see — on tickets, in community member lists, anywhere your name appears. Change it from the Profile card whenever you want.

## Email address

Your email is how Braket reaches you for ticket confirmations, vetting decisions, and event updates.

To change it:

1. Enter your new email in the Email Address card
2. Click **Request Change**
3. Check your new inbox for a verification link
4. Click the link to confirm

Until you confirm, your old email stays active. You'll see a "Pending Verification" banner showing the new address. If you change your mind, hit **Cancel Request** and nothing changes.

## Social accounts

You can link Google and Discord to your Braket account for faster sign-in. The Social Accounts card shows which providers are connected and lets you link or unlink them.

If you signed up with Google or Discord originally, that provider is already linked. You can add the other one too, or unlink a provider if you no longer want to use it for sign-in.

You can't unlink your only sign-in method. If you signed up through a social provider and haven't set a password, you'll need to set one before you can disconnect that provider.

## Password

Change your password from the Password & Security card. Enter your current one and pick a new one.

If you signed up with Google or Discord and never set a password, you'll see a "Set Password" option instead of the change form. Setting a password gives you email-based sign-in as a backup alongside your social login.
