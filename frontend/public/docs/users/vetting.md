# Vetting

Most communities on Braket use vetting to protect their spaces.

## Why communities vet

Communities on Braket often center queer, trans, and marginalized people. Vetting is how they make sure the people coming to their events actually belong in those spaces. It's not a formality — it matters.

Each community writes its own vetting questions and makes its own decisions. What one community asks about might be completely different from another.

## How to apply

1. Find a community — through the community directory, a friend's invite link, or an event page
2. Open the community's page
3. Fill out the vetting application
4. Submit and wait for the admins to review it

The questions vary. Some communities ask two things, others ask six. Answer honestly — real people are reading your responses.

## Checking your status

Your dashboard shows the status of all your vetting applications. You'll see one of these:

- **Pending** — the admins haven't reviewed it yet. Hang tight.
- **Approved** — you're in \o/ You can now access that community's private events and buy tickets.
- **Rejected** — the admins decided not to approve you this time. They may include a reason.

You'll also get an email when a decision is made.

## What approval gets you

Once you're vetted into a community, you can see their private events and buy tickets. You'll also show up on the community's member list.

Vetting is per-community — being approved in one doesn't automatically get you into another, unless those communities have shared vetting set up.

## Shared vetting

Some communities trust each other and set up trust links. If community A trusts community B, then being vetted into B might get you automatically approved in A.

This happens behind the scenes. If a community you apply to has a trust link with one you're already in, you might get approved right away without anyone manually reviewing your application.

Not all communities use this, and trust links can be one-way. The admins decide.

## Magic links

Sometimes a community admin will send you an invite link directly. These "magic links" auto-vet you — click the link, and you're approved without filling out an application. Admins use these for people they already know and trust.
