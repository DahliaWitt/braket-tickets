# Buying Tickets

## Finding Events

Browse available events on the Events page. Public events are visible to everyone. Private events are only visible after you've been vetted into the hosting community.

## Purchasing

1. Select an event and choose your ticket tier
2. Complete payment via embedded Stripe Checkout
3. Your ticket and QR code will appear on your Tickets page

## At the Door

Show your QR code to the door scanner when you arrive. The scanner will verify your ticket in real-time.

## Resale

If you can't make it, you can list your ticket for resale. The next person on the waitlist will be notified automatically.
