# Buying tickets

## Find events

Browse the Events page to see what's coming up. What you can see depends on your access:

- **Public events** are visible to everyone, even if you're not signed in.
- **Public (viewable)** events are visible to everyone, but only vetted members can buy tickets.
- **Private events** only show up if you're vetted into the hosting community.

If you don't see many events, you might need to [get vetted](./vetting.md) into a community first.

## Choose your tickets

Once you open an event, you'll see the available ticket tiers and pricing. Some events use sliding scale pricing, so you can pay what feels right within the range the organizer set.

Pick your tier, set the quantity (up to the per-person limit), and head to checkout.

## Pay

Payment happens right on the page — you won't get redirected anywhere. Enter your card info in the payment form and confirm. Braket uses Stripe to process payments, so your card details never touch Braket's servers.

Free events skip the payment step entirely. Just grab your ticket.

If an event is sold out but has resale tickets available, you'll see those instead. Resale tickets are always at the original price, one at a time. See [Resale and waitlist](./resale-and-waitlist.md).

## No account? No problem

Public events support guest checkout. Enter your email, pay, and your ticket gets delivered to your inbox. No account creation needed.

Guest tickets can't be resold and won't show up in a dashboard, but they get the job done. If you want the full experience, [creating an account](./getting-started.md) takes about a minute.

More on this in [Guest checkout](./guest-checkout.md).

## After you buy

If you're signed in, your ticket and QR code show up on your Tickets page right away. Either way, you'll get a confirmation email too.

Hold onto that QR code — you'll need it at the door (^\_^)

## At the door

Show your QR code to the door staff when you arrive. They scan it, the system verifies your ticket in real time, and you're in. Each ticket can only be scanned once.

If you bought as a guest, pull up the ticket from your confirmation email instead.

## Can't make it?

List your ticket for resale if your plans change. Someone on the waitlist gets notified, and if they buy it, you get your money back. See [Resale and waitlist](./resale-and-waitlist.md).
