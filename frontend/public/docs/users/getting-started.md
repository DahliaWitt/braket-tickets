# Getting started

## Create your account

Head to the login page and sign up with your email, or connect with Google or Discord. Google and Discord skip the email verification step — you're in right away. Email signup sends a verification link to your inbox first.

You can update your display name, email, password, or linked accounts later from [Account Settings](./your-account.md).

## Find communities

Braket is built around communities. Each one runs its own events and handles its own membership.

Check out the community directory to see who's around. Some communities list themselves publicly; others are invite-only and won't show up there. If someone sends you an invite link, that link gets you in directly.

## Apply for vetting

Most communities use vetting to keep their spaces safe. It works like this: you answer a few questions the community's admins have set up, they read your answers, and they decide whether to let you in.

To apply:

1. Open a community's page, or follow a link to one of their events
2. Fill out the vetting application
3. Hang tight while the admins review it

You can check your status from your dashboard anytime. If you're approved, you get access to that community's private events. If you're denied, the admins can include a reason.

Some communities also share vetting through trust links — if you're already vetted in one community, a linked community might accept you automatically. More on that in the [Vetting](./vetting.md) guide.

## Buy tickets

Once you're vetted, you can grab tickets to a community's private events. Public events are open to everyone, no vetting needed. You can even check out as a [guest](./guest-checkout.md) on public events without making an account.

Head to the Events page to browse what's coming up \o/

See [Buying Tickets](./buying-tickets.md) for the full checkout flow.
