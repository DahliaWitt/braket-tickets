# Getting Started with Braket

Welcome to Braket — a ticketing and community vetting platform built by and for the underground community.

## Creating Your Account

1. Visit the login page and create an account with your email
2. Verify your email address via the confirmation link
3. Once verified, you can browse public events and apply for community vetting

## What is Vetting?

Many communities on Braket use a vetting process to protect their spaces. When you apply, community admins review your application to ensure the space stays safe for everyone.

## Buying Tickets

Once you're vetted into a community, you can purchase tickets to their private events. Some events are also open to the public — check the events page to see what's available.
