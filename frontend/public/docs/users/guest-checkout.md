# Guest checkout

Don't want to make an account? For public events, you don't have to. Guest checkout lets you buy a ticket with just your email address.

## How it works

1. Find a public event on the Events page
2. Choose your ticket tier and quantity
3. Enter your email address
4. Complete payment through the on-page checkout form
5. Check your email for your ticket and QR code

No password, no profile, no sign-up flow (^\_^)

## At the door

Pull up the ticket from your confirmation email and show the QR code to door staff. It works the same as any other ticket.

## What you can't do as a guest

Guest checkout is quick, but it comes with trade-offs.

Your ticket lives in your email — there's no Tickets page or dashboard to manage it from. You can't list a guest ticket for resale, and guest checkout only works for public events (private events need an account and community vetting). If something comes up after purchase, you'll need to reach out to the event organizer directly.

## When to make an account instead

If you're planning to attend more than one event, or the events you're eyeing are private, [creating an account](./getting-started.md) is worth the minute it takes. Dashboard, resale, and vetting stay together.
