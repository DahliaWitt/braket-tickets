# Frequently asked questions

## Do I need an account to buy tickets?

Not for public events. You can use [guest checkout](./guest-checkout.md) with just an email address. Private events require an account and community vetting.

## How do I get vetted into a community?

Find the community (through the directory, an event page, or a friend's link) and fill out their vetting application. The admins review it and you'll get a notification with their decision. Full details in the [Vetting](./vetting.md) guide.

## How long does vetting take?

Depends on the community. Some admins review applications within hours, others take a few days. You can check your status from your dashboard anytime.

## I was denied — can I reapply?

That's up to the community. If the admins included a denial reason, read it carefully. Some communities allow reapplication, others don't.

## Where's my ticket?

If you have an account, check your Tickets page. If you checked out as a guest, your ticket was emailed to you. Check your spam folder if you don't see it.

## Can I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by each community's admins — there's no platform-wide refund policy. Reach out to the event organizer if you need one.

## I can't make it — can I give my ticket to someone else?

You can't transfer a ticket directly, but you can [list it for resale](./resale-and-waitlist.md). Someone else buys it at face value, you get your money back, and they get a fresh ticket.

## What's the waitlist?

If an event is sold out, you can subscribe to resale notifications. When someone lists a ticket for resale, you'll get an email. It's first come, first served — subscribing doesn't reserve anything. More in the [Resale and waitlist](./resale-and-waitlist.md) guide.

## How do I change my email or password?

From your [Account Settings](./your-account.md) page. Email changes require verification through your new inbox.

## Can I link Google or Discord to my account?

Yes — head to [Account Settings](./your-account.md) and look for the Social Accounts section. You can link and unlink Google and Discord there.

## What do I show at the door?

Your QR code. If you have an account, it's on your Tickets page. If you used guest checkout, pull it up from your confirmation email. Door staff scan it and you're in.

## I have a question that's not here

Reach out through the platform or contact the community that hosted the event. We're around \o/
