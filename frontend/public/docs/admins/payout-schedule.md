# Payout schedule

## How payouts work

When fans buy tickets through Braket Tickets, the money lands in your connected Stripe account. Braket holds the payout for 3 days after the event, then sends it to your bank. Nothing for you to do.

## Why 3 days?

Chargebacks and refund requests almost always show up in the first few days after an event. If we paid out immediately, we'd end up pulling money back out of your bank account when disputes came in. The hold avoids that.

Card networks also need time to finalize transactions. Three days covers both.

Most platforms hold for 5 to 7 days. Braket went with 3 because that still catches the majority of post-event issues. We do not want to sit on your money longer than we have to.

## Tracking your payout

On the Event Management page for any Stripe event, there's a status banner near the top.

Before the event, you'll see a note about the 3-day policy. After the event ends, it shows the date your payout is scheduled for. Once we've initiated the transfer, the banner updates. Usually takes 1 to 2 business days from there for the money to actually land. When it's done, you get a green confirmation with the date.

## When payouts go out

The system runs daily. Your event becomes eligible on day 4 (3-day hold, then the next scheduled run). Banks typically take another 1 to 2 business days after that.

## What can delay things

If you haven't finished Stripe setup, we can't send the money anywhere. You can complete it from your Community Admin settings. See [Set Up Stripe](./stripe-setup.md).

If chargebacks or refunds ate up the balance, there's nothing left to transfer. The Payments section in Community Admin shows your Stripe payment, balance, and payout details.

Stripe sometimes flags accounts for extra verification too. If that happens, you'll see prompts in the Payments section telling you what they need.

## Notifications

When your payout goes out, we email you the amount and event name with a link to the management page.
