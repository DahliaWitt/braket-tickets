# Vetting applications

The admin side of vetting — reviewing incoming applications and making decisions. For what applicants see on their end, see [Vetting](../users/vetting.md).

## Pending Apps tab

When someone applies to join your community, their application shows up in the **Pending Apps** tab of your Community Admin dashboard. Each application shows the applicant's name and their answers to your community's vetting questions.

Read through the answers, then make a call:

- **Approve** — the applicant becomes a vetted member and can access your community's private events.
- **Deny** — the applicant is rejected. You can (and should) include a reason so they understand why.

## Denial reasons

When you deny someone, you can include a reason. The applicant sees it on their dashboard and in their notification email. A clear, honest reason is better than silence — it lets people know where they stand and whether reapplying makes sense.

## App History tab

Every application you've processed moves to the **App History** tab. You can look back at past decisions, see who approved or denied each application, and review the original answers.

This is useful when someone reapplies, or if another admin wants to understand a past decision.

## Shared vetting

If your community has trust links with other communities, some applicants may be automatically approved based on their membership elsewhere. These auto-approved applications still show up in your history.

See [Shared vetting](./shared-vetting.md) for how to set up and manage trust links.

One more thing: check Pending Apps regularly. A slow response feels like a rejection to someone waiting to find out if they're welcome. You can update your vetting questions anytime from [Community settings](./community-settings.md) if the current ones aren't giving you useful answers.
