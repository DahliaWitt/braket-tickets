# Community settings

Everything lives under **Settings** in your Community Admin dashboard — one page, six sections.

## Profile

Name, slug (the URL path, like `/communities/your-slug`), description, website link, and logo.

The two toggles people miss: **directory listing** controls whether your community shows up in the public directory at `/communities`. Invite-only communities usually leave this off. **Status** sets whether the community is published or still in draft — draft communities aren't visible to anyone outside the admin dashboard.

## Code of conduct

Shown to applicants during vetting and to members on your community page. Write one that actually means something — people read this before deciding whether to apply, and it sets the tone for what your space is about.

## Payments

Your Stripe connection status lives here: whether charges are enabled, payouts are enabled, and whether Stripe still needs something from you. If you haven't connected yet, see [Set up Stripe](./stripe-setup.md).

Once connected, this section doubles as your Stripe home inside Braket — payment details, disputes, bank info. You can change where the money goes, but Braket controls when. See [Payout schedule](./payout-schedule.md).

## Vetting questions

The questions applicants answer when they apply. Add, remove, reorder anytime.

Think about what you actually need to know to make a decision. The answers are what you'll be reading in the Pending Apps tab, so vague questions get vague answers. Some communities ask two questions. Others ask six. There's no right number — just make sure each one earns its place.

One thing to know: changes apply to new applications only. Existing applications keep whatever questions were active when they were submitted.

## Team management

Who has admin access and who can scan tickets at the door. Covered in [Team management](./team-management.md).

## Notifications

Which email alerts your admins get — new vetting applications, mostly. Worth turning on so applications don't sit unreviewed for days.
