# Magic links

Magic links are invite URLs that auto-vet anyone who clicks them. For people you already know and trust — skip the application, skip the review, just get them in.

## When to use them

Good fits for magic links:

- Someone you know personally who you want at your event
- A batch of people from a trusted space who you want to onboard quickly
- Artists, staff, or collaborators who need community access without the application process

If you're not sure whether someone should be vetted, let them go through the normal application. That's what it's there for.

## Creating a magic link

1. Go to the **Magic Links** tab in your Community Admin dashboard
2. Click to create a new link
3. Share the URL with whoever you want to invite

When someone clicks the link, they'll be prompted to sign in or create an account (if they don't have one), and then they're automatically vetted into your community.

## Managing links

The Magic Links tab shows all your active and expired links. You can see when each was created and how it's been used.

Links you no longer need can be deactivated. Once deactivated, the URL stops working — anyone who clicks it won't be auto-vetted.

## How it looks on their end

The person clicking the link lands on a page that either signs them in or prompts account creation, then confirms they've been added to your community. The whole thing takes about ten seconds.

They show up in your Members list right away. They won't appear in App History since they never submitted an application — magic link redemptions are logged in the Audit Log instead.
