# Frequently asked questions

## Do I need Stripe to create events?

Only if you're charging for tickets. Free events don't need Stripe at all. If you want to sell paid tickets, see [Set up Stripe](./stripe-setup.md).

## When do I get paid?

Braket holds payouts for 3 days after your event, then sends them to your bank via Stripe. Banks usually take another 1-2 business days after that. Full breakdown in [Payout schedule](./payout-schedule.md).

## Can I have multiple admins?

Yes. Invite co-admins from [Settings → Team](./team-management.md). Every admin gets full dashboard access. Be thoughtful about who you add — any admin can invite or remove other admins.

## How do I add door staff?

Door staff (scanners) are assigned from [Settings → Team](./team-management.md). They can scan QR codes at the door but can't access the admin dashboard.

## Can I issue refunds?

Yes, from the **Buyers** tab on any event's management page. You can refund individual tickets or full orders. See [Refunds](./refunds.md).

## What's the difference between private and public events?

Private events are only visible to vetted community members. Public events are visible to everyone and can be purchased without vetting. There's also "Public (viewable)" — visible to everyone but only purchasable by vetted members. See [Managing events](./managing-events.md) for the full breakdown.

## How do I change my vetting questions?

Go to [Settings](./community-settings.md) and scroll to Vetting Questions. You can add, remove, and reorder questions anytime. Changes apply to new applications only — existing ones keep the original questions.

## What are trust links?

Trust links let you auto-approve applicants who are already vetted in another community. Set them up from the [Shared Vetting](./shared-vetting.md) tab. They're one-way — you can trust another community without them trusting you back.

## How do magic links work?

Magic links are invite URLs that auto-vet anyone who clicks them. Create them from the [Magic Links](./magic-links.md) tab when you want to skip the application process for someone you already trust.

## Can I cancel an event?

Yes. Cancelled events are removed from public listings but stay in your admin dashboard. Existing ticket holders can see the cancellation. You'll want to handle refunds manually from the Buyers tab.

## How do I send emails to ticket holders?

From the **Email** tab on the event management page. You can broadcast to all ticket holders — useful for venue changes, schedule updates, or pre-event hype.

## Where can I see what my admins have been doing?

The **Audit Log** tab in your Community Admin dashboard tracks admin actions for accountability.
