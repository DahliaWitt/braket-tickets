# Team management

You don't have to run everything yourself. Braket has two roles you can hand out: admins and door staff.

**Admins** get the full Community Admin dashboard — vetting, events, settings, refunds, the works. They can do anything you can do, including inviting or removing other admins. Only give this to people you trust with the whole operation.

**Door staff** get one thing: the QR code scanner. They can check people in at the door but can't see applications, settings, financial data, or the member list. Good for friends helping out who don't need the admin view.

## Inviting admins

From **Settings → Team** in your Community Admin dashboard, click to invite, enter their email, and they get an invitation. Once they accept, they have full admin access.

## Assigning door staff

Also from Settings → Team. Once someone is assigned as door staff, they can scan tickets at any of your community's events.

## Removing access

Revoke admin or door staff access from the same place. Takes effect immediately — no grace period, no confirmation email. They just lose access.

## A note on trust

Any admin can invite or remove any other admin. There's no hierarchy, no "owner" role. The Audit Log tracks who did what, so if something looks off you can trace it back.
